I’m someone who values staying connected to the world around me, finding magic in nature, music, and the people in my life. On weekends, I’m usually recharging—either hitting a hiking trail, going for a leisurely walk, or spending time working on my mom's or gram's house. Incorporating movement, stretching, and dance is something I'm turning into a daily practice. Don't get me wrong though, I’m just as happy having a night in, binge-watching trash TV like 90 Day Fiancé.

I'm just a person who works hard to embody my values. I thrive in spaces where community comes first, and I try to bring that same dedication and purposeful energy to everything I do and everyone I care about as I advocate for a more compassionate world.
